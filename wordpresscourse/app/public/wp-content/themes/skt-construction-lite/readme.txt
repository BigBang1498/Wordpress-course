== Description ==

SKT Construction is a construction, architecture, builder, and industry related business, corporate, personal, photography and real estate theme which showcases project galleries, contact info, nicely laid out content, animated sections, and compatibility with WooCommerce, NextGen Gallery and contact form 7.
